# Tally Prime & GST Mastery Path

A free, structured, module-wise TallyPrime and GST accounting training program — entry level to master level, with step-by-step instructions, shortcut cheat sheets, worked figures and a hands-on exercise in every module.

**Live site:** https://learnmasterypath.com/tally-gst-mastery-path/

## Contents

- **Level 1 · Entry** — Golden rules, TallyPrime basics, ledgers and groups, vouchers, sales and purchases, inventory, bank reconciliation, core reports, GST fundamentals
- **Level 2 · Intermediate** — GST setup, GST invoicing, ITC and GSTR-2B, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, credit and debit notes, e-invoicing and e-way bills, RCM and composition, bill-wise tracking, cost centres
- **Level 3 · Advanced** — TDS, TCS, payroll with PF and ESI, advanced inventory, manufacturing, banking utilities, foreign currency and exports, year-end adjustments, GSTR-9
- **Level 4 · Master** — Ratio analysis, MIS reporting, internal controls and audit trail, data management, compliance calendar, MSME accounting, moving to ERP, practice and freelancing, career
