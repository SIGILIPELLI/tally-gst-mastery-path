    Tally Prime & GST Cheat Sheet¶

    Quick-reference tables from 36 lessons of the free Tally Prime & GST Mastery Path, in course order from beginner to advanced. Each section links to the full lesson with explanations and runnable examples.

    Level 1 · Entry¶

    01 · Accounting Fundamentals & the Golden Rules¶

    Account type Debit Credit
    Personal Receiver Giver
    Real What comes in What goes out
    Nominal Expenses & losses Incomes & gains
    Equation Assets = Liabilities + Capital
    Check Trial Balance totals match

    02 · Getting Started with TallyPrime¶

    Shortcut Action
    Alt+K Company menu (create, select, alter)
    Alt+G Go To (search any screen)
    Ctrl+A Accept / save
    Esc Back / cancel
    Alt+C Create master on the fly
    F2 / Alt+F2 Change date / period
    F11 Company features
    F12 Configure screen
    Alt+P / Alt+E Print / Export

    03 · Ledgers & Groups¶

    Ledger Correct group
    Owner's investment Capital Account
    Bank account Bank Accounts
    Customer Sundry Debtors
    Supplier Sundry Creditors
    GST/TDS payable Duties & Taxes
    Office rent, salary Indirect Expenses
    Freight inward, factory wages Direct Expenses
    Furniture, computer Fixed Assets

    04 · Payment, Receipt, Contra & Journal Vouchers¶

    Voucher Key Use
    Contra F4 Cash ↔ bank, bank ↔ bank
    Payment F5 Money out (cash/bank)
    Receipt F6 Money in (cash/bank)
    Journal F7 Non-cash adjustments
    Delete / Cancel / Duplicate Alt+D / Alt+X / Alt+2

    05 · Sales & Purchase Vouchers¶

    Task Path / key
    Purchase F9
    Sales F8
    Accounting vs item invoice Ctrl+H (Change Mode)
    Supplier invoice no. Enter on every purchase
    Print invoice Alt+P
    Delivery / Receipt Note Alt+F8 / Alt+F9
    Order processing F11

    06 · Inventory Basics: Stock Items, Groups & Units¶

    Master / voucher Path or key
    Unit Create Master → Unit
    Stock Group / Category Create Master → Stock Group / Category
    Stock Item Create Master → Stock Item
    Stock Journal Alt+F7
    Physical Stock Ctrl+F7
    Stock Summary Alt+G → Stock Summary

    07 · Bank Reconciliation¶

    Situation Action
    Entry in both books & statement Set bank date
    In books, not in statement Leave unreconciled (timing)
    In statement, not in books Create voucher, then reconcile
    Error in books Alter voucher
    Auto match Import statement, review matches

    08 · Core Reports: Day Book, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet¶

    Report Answers
    Day Book What was entered today/this period?
    Ledger Full history of one account
    Group Summary Balances of all customers/suppliers
    Trial Balance Do debits equal credits? Any odd balances?
    Profit & Loss Gross and net profit for a period
    Balance Sheet Financial position on a date

    09 · GST Fundamentals: CGST, SGST, IGST & Registration¶

    Term Meaning
    Output tax GST on sales
    ITC Credit for GST paid on purchases
    CGST + SGST Intra-state supply
    IGST Inter-state supply, imports
    HSN / SAC Codes for goods / services
    GSTIN 15-character registration number
    GSTR-1 / 3B Sales details / summary + payment

    Level 2 · Intermediate¶

    01 · GST Setup in TallyPrime¶

    Setting Where
    Enable GST, GSTIN, state, periodicity F11 / Alt+K → GST
    Tax ledgers Duties & Taxes, Type = GST, Tax type
    Sales/Purchase ledger GST applicable
    Party details Registration type, GSTIN, state
    Item details HSN/SAC, taxability, rate
    Bulk rate review GST Rate Setup

    02 · GST Invoicing: HSN/SAC, Tax Rates & Place of Supply¶

    Scenario Tax Notes
    Same state, registered buyer CGST + SGST B2B in GSTR-1
    Different state, registered IGST Place of supply = buyer state
    Unregistered, inter-state > ₹1 lakh IGST B2CL
    Unregistered, other As applicable B2CS summary
    Purchase Input CGST/SGST or IGST Match supplier invoice no.

    03 · Input Tax Credit & GSTR-2B Reconciliation¶

    Check Rule
    Documents Valid invoice, goods/services received
    Supplier compliance In GSTR-2B, tax paid
    Time limit 30 Nov of next FY (or annual return date)
    Payment Within 180 days or reverse
    Blocked Sec 17(5) items
    Monthly Review IMS, reconcile 2B with books

    04 · GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B Returns¶

    Return Content Due (monthly)
    GSTR-1 Invoice-level sales 11th
    GSTR-3B Summary + tax payment 20th
    IFF B2B invoices (QRMP months 1–2) 13th
    PMT-06 Monthly payment (QRMP) 25th
    Set-off IGST → CGST/SGST; CGST ↛ SGST

    05 · Credit Notes, Debit Notes & Sales Returns¶

    Situation Voucher Key
    Sales return Credit Note Ctrl+F8
    Post-sale discount (pre-agreed, linked) Credit Note Ctrl+F8
    Undercharged / extra charges Debit Note Ctrl+F9
    Purchase return (record) Debit Note Ctrl+F9
    Always reference Original invoice no. & date
    Time limit 30 Nov of next FY

    06 · E-Invoicing & E-Way Bills¶

    Item Rule (2026)
    E-invoice threshold AATO > ₹5 crore
    Output IRN + signed QR on invoice
    30-day reporting AATO ≥ ₹10 crore
    IRN cancellation Within 24 hours
    E-way bill threshold Consignment > ₹50,000 (state variations)
    E-way validity ~1 day per 200 km

    07 · Reverse Charge & the Composition Scheme¶

    Topic Key rule
    RCM payer Recipient
    RCM payment Cash only; ITC claimable if eligible
    Self-invoice For purchases from unregistered under RCM
    GSTR-3B 3.1(d) liability, 4 ITC
    Composition limit ₹1.5 crore (goods), ₹50 lakh (services)
    Composition rates 1% / 5% / 6%
    Composition returns CMP-08 quarterly, GSTR-4 annual

    08 · Bill-wise Details: Receivables & Payables¶

    Concept Use
    New Ref New invoice
    Agst Ref Payment against a bill
    Advance Paid/received before invoice
    On Account Unlinked (reconcile later)
    Receivables / Payables Pending bills, overdue days
    Ageing 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+
    DSO Receivables ÷ credit sales × days

    09 · Cost Centres & Budgets¶

    Feature Path
    Enable F11 → Cost Centres / Budgets
    Category Dimension (branch, person, project)
    Allocation In voucher, ledgers with cost centres applicable
    Classes Auto-allocation rules
    Reports Cost Centre Break-up, Category Summary
    Budget Create Master → Budget; variance in reports

    Level 3 · Advanced¶

    01 · TDS: Deduction, Payment & Returns¶

    Step Rule
    Deduct At credit or payment, whichever earlier
    No PAN Higher rate
    Deposit 7th of next month (30 April for March)
    Returns Quarterly: 31 Jul, 31 Oct, 31 Jan, 31 May
    Certificates From TRACES
    TallyPrime F11 TDS, nature of payment, party PAN, Stat Payment

    02 · TCS & Other Withholdings¶

    Withholding Who collects Rate (examples) Report
    Income-tax TCS Seller Scrap 1%, motor vehicle > ₹10 lakh 1% Quarterly TCS statement
    LRS TCS Bank/forex dealer 20% above ₹10 lakh (certain purposes) —
    GST TCS E-commerce operator 0.5% GSTR-8
    E-commerce TDS (income tax) Operator 0.1% Operator's TDS return

    03 · Payroll: Salary, PF & ESI¶

    Item Rule (2026)
    PF 12% employee + 12% employer (8.33% EPS capped at ₹1,250)
    PF wage ceiling ₹15,000 (mandatory)
    ESI 0.75% employee + 3.25% employer, wages ≤ ₹21,000
    PF/ESI due 15th of next month
    Payroll voucher Ctrl+F4
    Attendance Ctrl+F5

    04 · Advanced Inventory: Batches, Godowns & Valuation¶

    Feature Use
    Godowns Stock by location, transfers
    Batches & expiry Traceability, FEFO
    Costing method Average, FIFO, last purchase
    Reorder levels Automated purchase suggestions
    Negative stock warning Prevent overselling
    Price levels Different selling prices

    05 · Manufacturing: Bill of Materials & Job Work¶

    Task Voucher
    Define recipe BoM in stock item
    Production Stock Journal → Manufacture
    Add labour/overheads Additional cost in manufacture
    Send to job worker Job Work Out Order → Material Out
    Receive back Material In + job worker invoice
    Process for others Job Work In Order → Material In/Out
    GST report ITC-04

    06 · Banking: Cheques, e-Payments & Post-Dated Cheques¶

    Utility Purpose
    Cheque printing Print cheques from payment vouchers
    Cheque Register Track cheque numbers and status
    e-Payments Export NEFT/RTGS bank files
    Post-Dated Summary Upcoming cheques for cash planning
    Deposit Slip Bank deposit list
    Payment Advice Inform parties of payments

    07 · Foreign Currency & Exports¶

    Item Treatment
    Currency setup F11 multi-currency, rates of exchange
    Export under LUT No IGST; refund ITC
    Export with IGST Pay IGST; refund
    Forex gain/loss Realised on receipt; restate at year-end
    Import IGST Paid at customs; claim ITC
    Customs duty Added to inventory cost

    08 · Year-End Adjustments: Depreciation & Provisions¶

    Adjustment Entry
    Depreciation Dr Depreciation / Cr Asset
    Outstanding expense Dr Expense / Cr Outstanding (liability)
    Prepaid expense Dr Prepaid (asset) / Cr Expense
    Accrued income Dr Accrued (asset) / Cr Income
    Doubtful debts Dr Provision expense / Cr Provision
    Stock write-down Value at lower of cost and NRV

    09 · GSTR-9 Annual Return & Audit Readiness¶

    Return Who Due
    GSTR-9 Regular taxpayers (optional ≤ ₹2 crore) 31 Dec
    GSTR-9C AATO > ₹5 crore With GSTR-9
    DRC-03 Voluntary payment of additional tax Before filing
    Records Retain 72 months
    Reconciliation pack Books vs returns vs portal Annual (monthly better)

    Level 4 · Master¶

    01 · Financial Statement Analysis & Ratios¶

    Category Ratios
    Profitability Gross margin, net margin, ROE
    Liquidity Current ratio, quick ratio
    Efficiency Debtor, inventory, creditor days; cash conversion cycle
    Solvency Debt-to-equity, interest coverage
    Tool Alt+G → Ratio Analysis

    02 · MIS Reports for Management¶

    MIS section TallyPrime source
    P&L vs budget P&L with columns + budget
    Sales analysis Sales Register, Cost Centre, Movement Analysis
    Receivables/payables Bills Receivable/Payable, ageing
    Cash forecast Cash/Bank books + Post-Dated Summary
    Stock Stock Summary, ageing, reorder status
    Distribution Export to Excel/PDF, email

    03 · Internal Controls, Security & the Edit Log¶

    Control TallyPrime feature
    Access Users, roles, security levels
    Encryption TallyVault
    Audit trail Edit Log
    Closed periods Lock period / restrict back-dating
    Review Day Book, edit log reports
    Segregation Separate roles for entry, approval, reconciliation

    04 · Data Management: Backup, Restore & Split¶

    Task Path
    Data path Alt+Y → Data path settings
    Backup Alt+Y → Backup
    Restore Alt+Y → Restore (test path first)
    Repair Alt+Y → Repair (after backup)
    Split year Alt+Y → Split
    Export/Import Alt+E / Alt+O

    05 · The Annual Compliance Calendar¶

    Area Key deadlines
    GST 11th (GSTR-1), 20th (GSTR-3B), 31 Dec (GSTR-9)
    TDS/TCS 7th deposit; quarterly statements
    Payroll 15th PF/ESI
    Income tax Advance tax quarterly; ITR by due date
    Company law AGM, AOC-4, MGT-7
    Tool Calendar + compliance tracker

    06 · Accounting for Startups & MSMEs¶

    Topic Key point
    Structure Proprietorship / LLP / Pvt Ltd trade-offs
    Separation Business bank account; drawings for personal use
    Founder loans Unsecured loans, documented
    Udyam Free MSME registration; benefits
    MSME payments Within 45 days; tax disallowance if late
    Runway Cash ÷ net monthly burn

    07 · Moving to an ERP: Tally, Zoho, SAP & Oracle¶

    Step Key action
    Decide Business need, not trend
    Requirements Must/should/nice-to-have
    Cost 5-year TCO
    Demo Your real scenarios
    Migrate Clean data, masters, opening balances
    Validate Parallel run + reconciliations

    08 · Running an Accounting Practice¶

    Area Practice
    Services Bookkeeping, GST, TDS, payroll, MIS, Tally setup
    Boundaries Audit/certification via CAs
    Engagement letter Scope, responsibilities, fees, confidentiality
    Workflow Checklists, trackers, review
    Security Remote access, encryption, minimal access
    Growth Niche, referrals, value-added MIS

    09 · Career Paths: Accountant to Finance Manager¶

    Role Core responsibilities
    Junior accountant Vouchers, reconciliation
    Accountant Books, GST, TDS, payroll
    Senior accountant Returns, audits, MIS
    Finance manager Budgets, cash, team, compliance
    R2R/GCC roles Close, reconciliations on ERPs
    Proof Projects, quantified resume, practical tests
