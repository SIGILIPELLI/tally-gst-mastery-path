# 01 · Accounting Fundamentals & the Golden Rules

Before touching TallyPrime, you need the logic it automates: **double-entry accounting**. Every transaction affects at least two accounts — one debited, one credited, for equal amounts. This module teaches the account types, the golden rules and journal entries using our practice business for Level 1: *Sri Sai Traders*, a stationery wholesaler in Hyderabad.

## 1. Key terms

1. **Capital** — money the owner puts into the business.
2. **Assets** — what the business owns: cash, bank balance, stock, furniture, amounts customers owe (**debtors**).
3. **Liabilities** — what it owes: loans, amounts owed to suppliers (**creditors**).
4. **Income** — sales, commission received, interest received.
5. **Expenses** — purchases, rent, salaries, electricity.
6. The **accounting equation**: Assets = Liabilities + Capital. Every correct entry keeps it balanced.

## 2. Three types of accounts and the golden rules

1. **Personal accounts** (people and organisations: Ramesh, HDFC Bank, Capital): **Debit the receiver, credit the giver.**
2. **Real accounts** (things: cash, stock, furniture, buildings): **Debit what comes in, credit what goes out.**
3. **Nominal accounts** (expenses, losses, incomes, gains): **Debit all expenses and losses, credit all incomes and gains.**

## 3. Worked journal entries

1. Owner starts business with ₹5,00,000 cash: Cash comes in (real → Dr), Capital is the giver (personal → Cr).
   `Cash A/c Dr 5,00,000 / To Capital A/c 5,00,000`
2. Deposits ₹3,00,000 in bank: Bank receives (personal → Dr), cash goes out (real → Cr).
   `Bank A/c Dr 3,00,000 / To Cash A/c 3,00,000`
3. Buys stationery stock on credit from Lakshmi Paper Mart for ₹80,000: Purchases (nominal expense → Dr), supplier gives (personal → Cr).
   `Purchases A/c Dr 80,000 / To Lakshmi Paper Mart 80,000`
4. Sells goods to Vijay Book Stall on credit for ₹50,000: Vijay receives (Dr), sales income (Cr).
   `Vijay Book Stall Dr 50,000 / To Sales A/c 50,000`
5. Pays rent ₹15,000 by bank: expense (Dr), bank gives (Cr).
   `Rent A/c Dr 15,000 / To Bank A/c 15,000`
6. Receives ₹30,000 from Vijay by UPI: bank receives (Dr), Vijay gives (Cr).
   `Bank A/c Dr 30,000 / To Vijay Book Stall 30,000`

## 4. From journal to final accounts

1. **Journal** — chronological record of entries.
2. **Ledger** — each account's entries grouped together with a running balance.
3. **Trial Balance** — list of all ledger balances; total debits must equal total credits.
4. **Profit & Loss Account** — incomes minus expenses = profit or loss for the period.
5. **Balance Sheet** — assets, liabilities and capital at a date.
6. TallyPrime does steps 2–5 automatically from your vouchers — but only if each entry is classified correctly.

## How It Actually Works

Double entry works because every transaction is an exchange: something of value moves from one place to another, so recording both sides keeps the books self-checking. If debits don't equal credits, an error exists. The golden rules are a memory aid for the deeper modern rule — assets and expenses increase with debits; liabilities, capital and income increase with credits — which is exactly how TallyPrime's groups decide where each ledger appears in the reports.
