# 10 · Project — One Month of Books for a Trading Business

Put Level 1 together: record a full month of transactions for a new trading business in TallyPrime and produce its reports. (GST is configured in Level 2, so this month uses amounts excluding tax.)

## 1. Company setup

1. Create **Kaveri Kitchenware** (Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu), financial year from 1 April 2026, with inventory enabled.
2. Create ledgers: Capital – Anitha (₹6,00,000 Cr), Canara Bank, Cash, Rent, Salary, Electricity, Freight Inward, Discount Allowed, Interest Received, Furniture, Sales, Purchases, Salary Payable; customers: Home Needs Store, Annapoorna Hotel; suppliers: Prestige Distributors, Steelcraft Industries.
3. Create units (Nos, Set), stock groups (Cookware, Utensils) and items: Pressure Cooker 5L (opening 40 @ ₹1,450), Non-stick Tawa (60 @ ₹520), Steel Plate Set (30 sets @ ₹780).

## 2. Transactions for the month

1. Capital ₹6,00,000: ₹4,50,000 deposited in Canara Bank, ₹1,50,000 cash (opening + Contra).
2. Bought furniture ₹38,000 by bank transfer.
3. Purchased 50 pressure cookers @ ₹1,420 from Prestige Distributors on credit; freight inward ₹1,200 cash.
4. Purchased 40 tawas @ ₹500 from Steelcraft Industries on credit.
5. Sold 30 pressure cookers @ ₹1,950 and 25 tawas @ ₹720 to Home Needs Store on credit.
6. Sold 12 steel plate sets @ ₹1,100 to Annapoorna Hotel for cash.
7. Received ₹40,000 from Home Needs Store by NEFT, allowing ₹500 discount.
8. Paid Prestige Distributors ₹60,000 by cheque.
9. Paid rent ₹18,000 and electricity ₹2,900 by bank.
10. Salary for the month ₹30,000: ₹20,000 paid in cash, ₹10,000 outstanding.
11. Interest credited by bank ₹350; bank charges ₹236.
12. Physical count: 2 tawas damaged — record via Stock Journal to a "Damaged Stock" item or as a loss.

## 3. Reconcile and review

1. Reconcile Canara Bank assuming the ₹60,000 cheque hasn't been presented yet.
2. Review the Day Book; fix any errors.
3. Check the Trial Balance balances and has no odd balances.

## 4. Reports to produce

1. Stock Summary with closing quantities and values.
2. Profit & Loss Account — note gross profit and net profit.
3. Balance Sheet at month end.
4. Outstanding balances of customers and suppliers (Group Summary).
5. Export all reports to PDF/Excel.

## How It Actually Works

This project mirrors a real month-end: transactions flow in from bank, cash, sales and purchases; reconciliation and stock counts verify them against independent evidence; and the reports summarise performance and position. The order matters — reconciling and checking the Trial Balance *before* reading profit ensures the numbers you report are trustworthy. Employers testing Tally skills usually give exactly this kind of task.
