# Level 1 · Entry <span class="level-badge">Foundations</span>

Goal: learn double-entry accounting, set up a company in TallyPrime, record everyday transactions, and finish with a month of books for a small trading business.

10. [Project — One Month of Books for a Trading Business](10-project-one-month-of-books-for-a-trading-business.md)

By the end of this level you'll apply the golden rules of accounting, create a company, ledgers and stock items, record payment, receipt, contra, journal, sales and purchase vouchers, reconcile a bank account, read the Trial Balance, P&L and Balance Sheet, explain how GST works — and you'll have a full month of books for a trading business.
