# 09 · GSTR-9 Annual Return & Audit Readiness

The GST year ends with the **annual return (GSTR-9)** and, for larger taxpayers, the **reconciliation statement (GSTR-9C)**. This module prepares both and builds the documentation that keeps audits and departmental notices manageable. Thresholds as of 2026 — check current notifications.

## 1. Who files what

1. **GSTR-9**: regular taxpayers file annually by **31 December** following the financial year. Filing is currently optional for taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to **₹2 crore**.
2. **GSTR-9C** (self-certified reconciliation between audited financial statements and GSTR-9): required for taxpayers with aggregate turnover above **₹5 crore**.
3. Composition taxpayers file **GSTR-4**; e-commerce operators GSTR-9B; others have specific forms.

## 2. GSTR-9 structure

1. **Part II** — outward and inward supplies declared during the year (from GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B).
2. **Part III** — ITC availed (from GSTR-3B), split into inputs, capital goods, input services; ITC reversed; ITC as per GSTR-2B (Table 8 comparison).
3. **Part IV** — tax paid as declared.
4. **Part V** — transactions of the year declared in the next year's returns (up to the cut-off), amendments.
5. **Part VI** — other info: demands/refunds, HSN-wise summary of outward and inward supplies, late fee.
6. Many fields are auto-populated on the portal; you review, reconcile and explain differences.

## 3. Prepare in TallyPrime

1. **Alt+G → GSTR-9** (or annual computation report) for the year.
2. Compare TallyPrime's annual figures with the portal's auto-populated GSTR-9 and with the sum of filed GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.
3. Common differences: invoices missed in GSTR-1 but included in GSTR-3B, credit notes, wrong tax heads (CGST/SGST vs IGST), ITC claimed vs available in GSTR-2B, ITC reversals.
4. Pay any additional liability discovered (with interest) through **DRC-03** before filing.
5. Export data and file on the portal with DSC/EVC.

## 4. Audit readiness

1. **Monthly discipline** prevents year-end surprises: GSTR-2B reconciliation, books vs returns comparison, prompt credit notes.
2. Maintain records for at least **72 months** from the due date of the annual return: invoices, credit/debit notes, e-way bills, delivery challans, ITC documents, payment proofs.
3. Keep a **reconciliation pack**: turnover per books vs GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B vs e-invoices vs e-way bills; ITC per books vs GSTR-2B vs GSTR-3B.
4. Respond to portal notices (e.g. ASMT-10 scrutiny, DRC-01 demands) within deadlines with reconciliations and documents.
5. Use TallyPrime's **edit log** (Level 4) to show the history of changes to vouchers if questioned.

## How It Actually Works

GSTR-9 consolidates a year of monthly declarations so the tax department can compare them with each other, with suppliers' data and — via GSTR-9C — with audited financial statements. Differences that slipped through monthly returns surface here, which is why the annual return often triggers corrections, additional payments or notices. A business that reconciles monthly turns the annual return into a summary of already-verified figures.
