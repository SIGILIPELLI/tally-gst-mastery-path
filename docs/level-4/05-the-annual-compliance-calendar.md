# 05 · The Annual Compliance Calendar

Indian businesses face recurring deadlines across GST, income tax, TDS/TCS, payroll and company law. Missing them costs interest, late fees and penalties — and damages credibility. This module builds an annual compliance calendar. Dates are typical as of 2026; confirm current due dates and any extensions on official portals.

## 1. Monthly

| Due | Compliance |
|---|---|
| 7th | Deposit TDS/TCS deducted in the previous month (March: 30 April) |
| 11th | GSTR-1 (monthly filers) |
| 13th | IFF / GSTR-1 (QRMP quarterly) |
| 15th | PF and ESI contributions and returns (ECR) |
| 20th | GSTR-3B (monthly filers) |
| 22nd/24th | GSTR-3B (QRMP, quarterly, state-wise) |
| 25th | PMT-06 GST payment (QRMP, months 1 and 2) |
| State dates | Professional tax payment/returns |

## 2. Quarterly

1. TDS/TCS statements: **31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May**; TCS statements a little earlier (15th after the quarter, historically).
2. TDS certificates to payees after statements are processed.
3. **Advance tax** instalments (if tax liability above threshold): 15 June (15%), 15 September (45%), 15 December (75%), 15 March (100%).
4. Composition dealers: **CMP-08** by the 18th after each quarter.
5. Review: GST reconciliation, receivables ageing, stock verification samples.

## 3. Annual

1. **GSTR-9/9C**: 31 December.
2. **Income tax returns**: non-audit cases typically 31 July; tax audit cases 31 October (tax audit report by 30 September); transfer pricing cases 30 November — check each year.
3. **Company law (ROC)**: AGM within 6 months of year-end (by 30 September); **AOC-4** (financial statements) within 30 days of AGM; **MGT-7/7A** (annual return) within 60 days of AGM; **DIR-3 KYC** for directors; **MSME-1** half-yearly if payments to MSMEs are overdue.
4. **LLP**: Form 11 (by 30 May) and Form 8 (by 30 October).
5. Statutory audit, stock verification, balance confirmations, fixed asset verification.
6. Renewals: trade licences, shop and establishment registration, professional tax enrolment, insurance.
7. GST: LUT for exports for the new year (before the first export), ITC-04 for job work as applicable.

## 4. Build and run the calendar

1. Create a calendar (Google Calendar/Outlook or a task tool) with recurring reminders 5 and 2 days before each deadline.
2. Assign an owner and a backup for each item.
3. Maintain a **compliance tracker**: period, due date, filed date, acknowledgement number, challan details, remarks.
4. Use TallyPrime reports (GST, TDS, payroll) to prepare data; keep acknowledgements and challans with the records.
5. Review monthly with the owner: filed, pending, any notices received.

## Cheat sheet

| Area | Key deadlines |
|---|---|
| GST | 11th (GSTR-1), 20th (GSTR-3B), 31 Dec (GSTR-9) |
| TDS/TCS | 7th deposit; quarterly statements |
| Payroll | 15th PF/ESI |
| Income tax | Advance tax quarterly; ITR by due date |
| Company law | AGM, AOC-4, MGT-7 |
| Tool | Calendar + compliance tracker |

## How It Actually Works

Each compliance date is the end of a chain of upstream work — accurate entries, reconciliations, approvals and payments — so the calendar is really a production schedule for the finance function. Businesses that miss deadlines usually fail upstream (late entries, unreconciled data), not on filing day. A tracker with acknowledgements also becomes the evidence pack when notices arrive, often years later.
