# 08 · Running an Accounting Practice

Many trained accountants serve multiple small businesses — as freelancers, bookkeeping firms or alongside a CA practice. This module covers services, pricing, client management, tools and professional boundaries.

## 1. Services you can offer

1. **Bookkeeping** in TallyPrime: monthly entries, bank reconciliation, receivables/payables.
2. **GST compliance**: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, reconciliations, e-invoicing setup, annual return preparation support.
3. **TDS compliance**: computations, deposits, quarterly statement preparation.
4. **Payroll processing**: salaries, PF/ESI, payslips.
5. **MIS reporting** and cash flow forecasts for owners.
6. **TallyPrime setup, migration and training** for businesses.
7. Note: certain services — statutory audits, tax audits, certification, some representations — are reserved for Chartered Accountants or other authorised professionals. Partner with a CA for those.

## 2. Pricing and engagement

1. Price by **package and volume**: e.g. monthly bookkeeping + GST for up to 150 transactions; add-ons for TDS, payroll, MIS.
2. Write an **engagement letter**: scope, client responsibilities (documents by the 5th), timelines, fees, payment terms, data confidentiality, limitations of responsibility.
3. Collect fees monthly in advance or by mandate; stop work policy for overdue fees.
4. Register for GST when your turnover crosses the threshold (₹20 lakh for services in most states) and issue proper invoices.

## 3. Workflow and tools

1. **Document collection**: shared folders (Google Drive/OneDrive), WhatsApp Business catalogues for checklists, or client portals; a monthly document checklist per client.
2. **Remote access**: TallyPrime remote access or cloud hosting to work on client data securely; never keep client data on personal devices without encryption.
3. **Task tracker** for every client and deadline (from Module 5's compliance calendar).
4. **Standard procedures**: month-end checklist, review by a second person, query lists sent to clients.
5. **Data security and confidentiality**: access only what's needed, strong passwords, backups, clear data-return terms when engagements end (DPDP obligations apply to personal data you handle).

## 4. Grow and retain clients

1. Niche by industry (restaurants, traders, clinics, startups) to work faster and market better.
2. Get clients through referrals from CAs, local business networks, LinkedIn, and helpful content (GST updates in plain language).
3. Deliver value beyond compliance: monthly MIS with insights, cash flow alerts, tax-saving reminders.
4. Communicate proactively about deadlines and changes; respond within an agreed time.

## How It Actually Works

An accounting practice is a production system: inputs (client documents) arrive on a schedule, processes (entry, reconciliation, review) convert them into outputs (books, returns, reports) by statutory deadlines. Standardisation — checklists, templates, niches — is what makes it profitable and reliable, because each client stops being a custom project. Clear engagement terms protect both sides when documents arrive late or responsibilities blur.
