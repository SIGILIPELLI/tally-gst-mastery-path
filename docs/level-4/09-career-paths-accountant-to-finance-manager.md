# 09 · Career Paths: Accountant to Finance Manager

TallyPrime and GST skills open many doors in India's accounting and finance job market. This module maps career paths, skills, qualifications and how to present your experience to employers.

## 1. Typical roles and progression

1. **Accounts Assistant / Junior Accountant** — data entry, vouchers, bank reconciliation, filing.
2. **Accountant** — full books, GST and TDS compliance, payroll, month-end close.
3. **Senior Accountant / Accounts Executive (GST/Taxation)** — reconciliations, returns, audit coordination, MIS.
4. **Accounts Manager / Finance Manager** — team leadership, budgeting, cash flow, statutory compliance, banking relationships.
5. **Financial Controller / CFO** (often with professional qualifications) — strategy, funding, governance.
6. Parallel paths: shared services and GCC roles (R2R, P2P, O2C processes on ERPs like SAP and Oracle), tax consulting, audit firms, freelancing/practice (Module 8).

## 2. Skills employers look for

1. TallyPrime proficiency including GST, TDS, payroll, inventory and reports.
2. GST end to end: invoicing, ITC reconciliation, GSTR-1/3B/9, e-invoicing, e-way bills.
3. Advanced **Excel**: lookups, pivot tables, reconciliation techniques (see the Excel Mastery Path).
4. Month-end close discipline and accuracy; documentation.
5. Communication with auditors, vendors and management; MIS commentary.
6. ERP exposure (SAP FICO, Oracle, Zoho) for larger companies.

## 3. Qualifications and certifications

1. **B.Com / M.Com / BBA** — the usual base.
2. Professional: **CA**, **CMA**, **CS** (India); **ACCA**, **CPA** (international roles).
3. Short certifications: Tally's official certification programmes, GST practitioner courses, SAP FICO training.
4. Experience with real books (internships, family business, practice firm) often matters as much as certificates.

## 4. Present your experience

1. Build a portfolio from this course: the projects (monthly books, GST month, quarter-end close, full-year capstone) with reports exported.
2. Write a results-focused resume: "Reconciled GSTR-2B monthly for 3 entities, recovering ₹4.2 lakh of missed ITC" or "Reduced month-end close from 10 days to 5."
3. Use the job's keywords honestly — GST, TDS, R2R, reconciliation, MIS, the ERP named — and tailor your resume to each role. The [Mastery Path Resume Tools](https://learnmasterypath.com/resume/) give you a free ATS keyword check against any job description.
4. Prepare for practical tests: record vouchers, compute GST and TDS, reconcile a bank statement in TallyPrime within a time limit.
5. Interview questions to expect: golden rules, ITC conditions, GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B, TDS sections and rates, depreciation methods, how you'd handle a supplier missing in GSTR-2B.

## How It Actually Works

Employers hire accountants to reduce risk and increase visibility: accurate books, on-time compliance and clear reports. Your career advances as you move from recording transactions to owning processes, then to interpreting results and advising decisions. Demonstrating that progression — with quantified examples and practical skill — is what distinguishes candidates in a market where many list "Tally" on their resume.
