# 10 · Capstone — A Full Year of Books & Compliance

Your capstone demonstrates complete mastery: a full financial year of books and compliance for a GST-registered business in TallyPrime, ending with year-end close, analysis and management reporting. Use *Annapurna Spices Pvt Ltd*, Indore (Madhya Pradesh), a manufacturer and trader of packaged spices with domestic and export sales — or a real business you're authorised to work on.

## 1. Setup (start of year)

1. Company with GST (monthly), e-invoicing (assume turnover above ₹5 crore), e-way bills, TDS, TCS, payroll, inventory with godowns and batches (with expiry dates), BOMs, multi-currency, cost centres (Plant, Sales, Admin), budgets.
2. Opening balances from the previous year's Balance Sheet (provided or created), including bill-wise receivables/payables and batch-wise stock.
3. Security roles and edit log enabled; backup policy in place; compliance calendar created.

## 2. Through the year (record at least one representative month per quarter in full, and summarise others)

1. Purchases (domestic, inter-state, import), production with BOMs, job work for packaging.
2. Sales (B2B intra/inter-state, B2C, exports under LUT, marketplace sales with GST TCS and TDS).
3. Credit/debit notes, RCM transactions, e-invoices and e-way bills.
4. Payroll with PF/ESI/PT; TDS on contractors, professionals and rent; TCS where applicable.
5. Banking: e-payments, PDCs, reconciliation every month.
6. Monthly GSTR-1/3B with GSTR-2B reconciliation; quarterly TDS statements; PF/ESI filings.
7. Monthly MIS packs against budget.

## 3. Year-end

1. Adjustments: depreciation (book and tax note), outstanding/prepaid, accrued income, provisions, stock verification and write-downs.
2. Forex restatement of open foreign balances.
3. GSTR-9 (and 9C data) reconciliation; ITC reversals for unpaid suppliers.
4. Final Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, cash flow, ratio analysis.
5. Lock the year, back up, and split the company into the new financial year.

## How It Actually Works

A full accounting year is the complete cycle every finance team runs: set up, record, reconcile, comply, adjust, close, report and roll forward. Each module of this course is one part of that cycle; the capstone shows you can run them together under real deadlines and dependencies. It's also your strongest proof for employers and clients — a documented year of books and compliance demonstrates far more than any certificate.
