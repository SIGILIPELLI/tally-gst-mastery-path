    Tally Prime & GST Interview Questions and Answers¶

    180 questions with the correct answer and a short explanation, organised from beginner (Level 1) to advanced (Level 4). Every question comes from a lesson in the free Tally Prime & GST Mastery Path, so you can open the lesson to go deeper.

    Practise, don't just read

    Each lesson also ends with an interactive quiz, and the final quiz tests all four levels. Try to answer each question below before reading the answer.

    Level 1 · Entry¶

    01 · Accounting Fundamentals & the Golden Rules¶

    Q1. What does the accounting equation state?

    Answer: Assets = Liabilities + Capital

    Every correct entry keeps it balanced.

    Q2. What is the golden rule for personal accounts?

    Answer: Debit the receiver, credit the giver

    Personal accounts are people and organisations such as Ramesh or HDFC Bank.

    Q3. The owner starts the business with ₹5,00,000 cash. What is the entry?

    Answer: Cash A/c Dr / To Capital A/c

    Cash comes in (real account, debit) and Capital is the giver (personal account, credit).

    Q4. You buy stationery stock on credit from Lakshmi Paper Mart for ₹80,000. What is the entry?

    Answer: Purchases A/c Dr / To Lakshmi Paper Mart

    Purchases is a nominal expense (debit) and the supplier is the giver (credit).

    02 · Getting Started with TallyPrime¶

    Q5. What is the limit in Educational Mode?

    Answer: Voucher dates are limited to the 1st, 2nd and 31st of a month

    All features work, which is enough for learning.

    Q6. Which shortcut opens a search box from anywhere in TallyPrime?

    Answer: Alt+G (Go To)

    Type 'Ledger', 'Sales' or 'Balance Sheet' and press Enter.

    Q7. How do you accept or save a screen?

    Answer: Ctrl+A

    Esc goes back, and Alt+C creates a master while inside a voucher.

    Q8. A TallyVault password is set on the company. What should you remember?

    Answer: It encrypts the data and cannot be recovered if forgotten

    Never forget it.

    03 · Ledgers & Groups¶

    Q9. What does a ledger's group decide?

    Answer: Where the ledger appears (Balance Sheet or Profit & Loss) and how it behaves

    Every ledger belongs to a group.

    Q10. Under which group does a customer like Vijay Book Stall belong?

    Answer: Sundry Debtors

    Suppliers go under Sundry Creditors.

    Q11. What happens if you classify freight inward as an indirect expense?

    Answer: Gross profit is overstated

    Direct expenses affect gross profit; indirect expenses affect net profit.

    Q12. Which two ledgers exist by default?

    Answer: Cash and Profit & Loss A/c

    Cash sits under Cash-in-Hand.

    04 · Payment, Receipt, Contra & Journal Vouchers¶

    Q13. You deposit ₹40,000 cash into HDFC Bank. Which voucher?

    Answer: Contra (F4)

    Contra records transfers between cash and bank accounts only.

    Q14. You pay rent of ₹15,000 from HDFC Bank. Which voucher?

    Answer: Payment (F5)

    Money is going out of the bank.

    Q15. Which entry is recorded in a Journal voucher?

    Answer: Depreciation on a computer, with no cash or bank involved

    Rule of thumb: cash/bank involved means Payment, Receipt or Contra; otherwise Journal.

    Q16. Why always write a narration?

    Answer: Auditors and your future self need to understand the transaction

    Narration explains each entry.

    05 · Sales & Purchase Vouchers¶

    Q17. Which function key opens a Sales voucher and which a Purchase voucher?

    Answer: F8 for Sales and F9 for Purchase

    F4 Contra, F5 Payment, F6 Receipt, F7 Journal, F8 Sales, F9 Purchase.

    Q18. How do you switch a voucher from accounting invoice to item invoice mode?

    Answer: Ctrl+H (Change Mode), with Maintain Inventory on

    Item invoice mode picks stock items, quantities and rates.

    Q19. A sale of 200 notebooks at ₹45 and 100 pen boxes at ₹60 totals what?

    Answer: ₹15,000

    200 × 45 = 9,000 plus 100 × 60 = 6,000.

    Q20. Why enter the supplier invoice number and date on a purchase voucher?

    Answer: It's important for GST and payments later

    The bill number links payments and GST records.

    06 · Inventory Basics: Stock Items, Groups & Units¶

    Q21. What does a compound unit such as 'Box of 10 Nos' let you do?

    Answer: Sell in boxes and track pieces

    Simple units are Nos, Box, Kg, Pcs and Ream.

    Q22. A stock item has opening quantity 500 at ₹38. What is its opening value?

    Answer: ₹19,000

    500 × 38 = 19,000.

    Q23. Which voucher records the actual counted quantities so book stock matches reality?

    Answer: Physical Stock voucher (Ctrl+F7)

    TallyPrime adjusts book stock to match the count.

    Q24. What is the default stock valuation method?

    Answer: Average Cost

    Other methods are set per item in Level 3.

    07 · Bank Reconciliation¶

    Q25. Why can books and bank statements differ on a given day?

    Answer: Uncleared cheques, unrecorded bank charges or interest, direct deposits and errors

    Reconciliation explains every difference.

    Q26. Bank charges of ₹590 include ₹500 plus 18% GST. How much is the GST?

    Answer: ₹90

    18% of 500 = 90.

    Q27. For each entry that appears in the bank statement, what do you enter in the reconciliation screen?

    Answer: The Bank Date, the date the bank processed it

    Entries without a bank date stay unreconciled.

    Q28. How often should you reconcile?

    Answer: At least monthly, ideally weekly for active accounts

    Frequent reconciliation keeps differences small.

    08 · Core Reports: Day Book, Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet¶

    Q29. What must be true of a Trial Balance?

    Answer: Total debits equal total credits

    A 'Difference in opening balances' line means opening balances don't agree.

    Q30. Which Trial Balance entry should look suspicious?

    Answer: Cash with a credit balance

    A credit balance in cash is impossible; also scan for suppliers with debit balances.

    Q31. How is Gross Profit computed in the example?

    Answer: Sales + closing stock minus opening stock, purchases and direct expenses

    The example gives cost of goods sold ₹78,700 and gross profit ₹56,300.

    Q32. Which report lists who owes how much?

    Answer: Group Summary of Sundry Debtors

    It lists all customers with balances.

    09 · GST Fundamentals: CGST, SGST, IGST & Registration¶

    Q33. Sri Sai Traders buys notebooks with ₹500 GST and sells them with ₹700 GST. What does it pay the government?

    Answer: ₹200

    Output tax ₹700 minus input tax credit ₹500.

    Q34. A Telangana seller sells to a customer in Hyderabad. Which tax applies?

    Answer: CGST + SGST

    Intra-state supply splits the tax into Central and State parts.

    Q35. A Telangana seller sells to Bengaluru, Karnataka. Which tax applies?

    Answer: IGST at the full rate

    Inter-state supply attracts IGST.

    Q36. Who ultimately bears the GST?

    Answer: The final consumer, with businesses acting as collectors

    Businesses collect output tax and claim input tax credit.

    10 · Project — One Month of Books for a Trading Business¶

    Q37. Why does the Level 1 project use amounts excluding tax?

    Answer: GST is configured in Level 2

    The month's transactions are recorded without GST.

    Q38. How do you record the owner's capital with ₹4,50,000 in the bank and ₹1,50,000 in cash?

    Answer: Capital credited ₹6,00,000, with bank and cash debited accordingly (using a Contra for the deposit)

    The brief says ₹4,50,000 deposited in Canara Bank and ₹1,50,000 cash.

    Q39. How should 2 damaged tawas found in the physical count be recorded?

    Answer: Through a Stock Journal to a 'Damaged Stock' item, or as a loss

    The project lists this as transaction 12.

    Q40. Why assume the ₹60,000 cheque hasn't been presented in the reconciliation?

    Answer: To practise treating cheques issued but not yet presented as a reconciling difference

    It appears in the books but not yet in the bank statement.

    Level 2 · Intermediate¶

    01 · GST Setup in TallyPrime¶

    Q41. Where do you enable GST for a company?

    Answer: F11 (Company Features), set Enable Goods and Services Tax to Yes

    Then enter state, registration type and GSTIN.

    Q42. Under which group are GST tax ledgers created?

    Answer: Duties & Taxes

    With Type of duty/tax set to GST and the matching tax type.

    Q43. Why leave the percentage blank on tax ledgers?

    Answer: TallyPrime picks the rate from the item or ledger GST details

    GST rates are best set on stock items.

    Q44. What do the first digits of a GSTIN such as 37ABCDE1234F1Z9 identify?

    Answer: The state (37 is Andhra Pradesh)

    TallyPrime also validates the GSTIN format.

    02 · GST Invoicing: HSN/SAC, Tax Rates & Place of Supply¶

    Q45. An intra-state sale of ₹12,000 at 18% shows what tax and invoice total?

    Answer: ₹1,080 CGST and ₹1,080 SGST; total ₹14,160

    9% CGST + 9% SGST on 12,000.

    Q46. An inter-state sale of ₹35,000 at 18% IGST has what total?

    Answer: ₹41,300

    IGST is 6,300.

    Q47. When is a Bill of Supply used instead of a tax invoice?

    Answer: When no tax is charged, e.g. by composition dealers or for exempt goods

    A tax invoice carries tax details.

    Q48. Where does TallyPrime take Place of Supply from by default?

    Answer: From the party's state, changeable if goods are delivered elsewhere

    For example 'bill to' one state and 'ship to' another.

    03 · Input Tax Credit & GSTR-2B Reconciliation¶

    Q49. Which is a condition for claiming ITC?

    Answer: The supplier has reported the invoice so it appears in your GSTR-2B

    You also need a valid tax invoice, receipt of goods or services, tax paid by the supplier and a filed GSTR-3B.

    Q50. If you don't pay the supplier within 180 days, what happens to the ITC?

    Answer: It must be reversed with interest and can be re-claimed when paid

    This is one of the claim conditions.

    Q51. What is GSTR-2B?

    Answer: A static monthly statement showing ITC available from suppliers' filings

    It's generated around the 14th and shows ITC available and not available.

    Q52. What does the Invoice Management System (IMS) let you do?

    Answer: Accept, reject or keep pending inward invoices before GSTR-2B is finalised

    Review it monthly.

    04 · GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B Returns¶

    Q53. What do GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B contain?

    Answer: GSTR-1 details of sales; GSTR-3B summary and payment

    Every regular taxpayer files both.

    Q54. Even with no activity in a month, what should a taxpayer do?

    Answer: File on time (nil returns can be filed by SMS)

    Late fees and interest apply otherwise.

    Q55. What should you resolve before exporting GSTR-1 from TallyPrime?

    Answer: Uncertain transactions such as missing GSTIN, HSN, place of supply or rate

    They appear in the return view.

    Q56. In tax set-off, which credit is used first?

    Answer: IGST credit against IGST liability, then CGST and SGST

    The worked example sets off IGST credit first.

    05 · Credit Notes, Debit Notes & Sales Returns¶

    Q57. How do you correct an issued GST invoice's tax value?

    Answer: Issue a credit or debit note; never edit the issued invoice

    Credit notes reduce value and debit notes increase it.

    Q58. A customer returns 5 kadais at ₹600 with 18% GST. What does the credit note total?

    Answer: ₹3,540

    ₹3,000 plus ₹270 CGST and ₹270 SGST.

    Q59. Why select the original invoice number and date in the credit note?

    Answer: Reference details are required for GST reporting

    It links the credit note to the invoice.

    Q60. A discount that wasn't agreed before supply or linked to invoices should be recorded how?

    Answer: As a commercial credit note without GST

    Such discounts don't reduce GST value.

    06 · E-Invoicing & E-Way Bills¶

    Q61. What does the IRP return when an e-invoice is registered?

    Answer: An IRN (Invoice Reference Number), acknowledgement details and a signed QR code

    TallyPrime stores and prints them on the invoice.

    Q62. Which invoices does e-invoicing apply to?

    Answer: B2B invoices, exports and credit/debit notes to registered persons, not B2C

    B2C may need a dynamic QR code above a higher threshold.

    Q63. Within what time can an IRN be cancelled?

    Answer: 24 hours; afterwards issue a credit note

    Cancel on the IRP, then in TallyPrime.

    Q64. What are Part A and Part B of an e-way bill?

    Answer: A: supplier/recipient and invoice details; B: transporter and vehicle details

    Validity is generally 1 day per 200 km for regular cargo.

    07 · Reverse Charge & the Composition Scheme¶

    Q65. What is the reverse charge mechanism?

    Answer: The recipient pays the GST instead of the supplier

    It applies to notified goods and services and some imports of services.

    Q66. How must tax under RCM be paid?

    Answer: In cash (electronic cash ledger), not with ITC

    The recipient can then claim the same amount as ITC if eligible.

    Q67. Advocate fees of ₹50,000 at 18% intra-state under RCM create what liability?

    Answer: CGST ₹4,500 and SGST ₹4,500

    The recipient pays ₹9,000 in cash and claims ₹9,000 ITC.

    Q68. Where is RCM liability reported in GSTR-3B?

    Answer: Table 3.1(d), with ITC in Table 4

    Liability and ITC are reported separately.

    08 · Bill-wise Details: Receivables & Payables¶

    Q69. What does 'Agst Ref' mean?

    Answer: A payment or receipt against an existing bill

    New Ref is a new bill; Advance is money before an invoice; On Account isn't linked to a bill.

    Q70. A receipt of ₹70,000 settles invoice A (₹59,000) fully and invoice B (₹35,400) partly. How much of B is pending?

    Answer: ₹24,400

    ₹70,000 − ₹59,000 = ₹11,000 against B; ₹35,400 − ₹11,000 = ₹24,400.

    Q71. What does ageing analysis group pending bills by?

    Answer: Age buckets such as 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days

    It identifies risky debts.

    Q72. Why does paying suppliers by due date matter for GST?

    Answer: It keeps credit terms and meets the 180-day rule for ITC

    Otherwise ITC may need reversing.

    09 · Cost Centres & Budgets¶

    Q73. What do cost centres tell you beyond total profit?

    Answer: Which branch, department, project or salesperson drove it

    A cost category is a dimension and cost centres sit under it.

    Q74. How can one voucher allocate sales by both branch and salesperson?

    Answer: Use different cost categories in the same voucher

    Branches and Salespeople are separate categories.

    Q75. What does a cost centre class do?

    Answer: Applies a fixed allocation automatically, such as salary split 50/30/20

    It saves repeated manual allocation.

    Q76. How do you compare actual results with a budget?

    Answer: Add the budget variance column in Trial Balance, Group Summary or Cost Centre reports

    It shows variance and percentage; review monthly.

    10 · Project — A GST-Compliant Month for a Wholesaler¶

    Q77. Which entry in the wholesaler project is paid in cash through the electronic cash ledger?

    Answer: Tax under reverse charge

    The project includes advocate fees and GTA freight under RCM.

    Q78. Why does the project create a GSTR-2B with one mismatch and one supplier non-filer?

    Answer: To practise reconciling purchases and deciding actions such as holding back ITC

    The summary must say how much ITC is held back and why.

    Q79. Which sales does the project make for GSTR-1 classification practice?

    Answer: Intra-state B2B, inter-state B2B, B2C retail and an inter-state B2C above ₹1 lakh

    The large inter-state B2C one is reported individually.

    Q80. What must the set-off journal and payment voucher reflect?

    Answer: Set-off in the correct order and RCM paid in cash

    It follows the GSTR-3B computation.

    Level 3 · Advanced¶

    01 · TDS: Deduction, Payment & Returns¶

    Q81. What is TDS?

    Answer: Tax deducted by the payer from certain payments and deposited with the government

    Examples: contractor bills, professional fees, rent, commission and salaries.

    Q82. What happens if the payee gives no valid PAN?

    Answer: TDS generally applies at a higher rate, often 20%

    Collect PAN before payment.

    Q83. By when is TDS generally deposited?

    Answer: The 7th of the following month (30 April for March)

    Quarterly statements follow on fixed dates.

    Q84. What do you need in order to deduct TDS?

    Answer: A TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number)

    TallyPrime asks for TAN when you enable TDS.

    02 · TCS & Other Withholdings¶

    Q85. How does TCS differ from TDS?

    Answer: TCS is collected by the seller from the buyer; TDS is deducted by the payer

    Both are deposited with the government.

    Q86. What GST TCS rate do e-commerce operators collect on net taxable supplies?

    Answer: 0.5% (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST, or 0.5% IGST)

    They report it in GSTR-8, and it appears in the seller's cash ledger.

    Q87. Where is a TCS payable ledger created?

    Answer: Under Duties & Taxes with Type TCS

    Set TCS applicability on the buyer ledger and sales ledger or item.

    Q88. When is income-tax TCS collected?

    Answer: At the time of receipt or debit to the buyer

    Deposit by the 7th of the following month and file quarterly statements.

    03 · Payroll: Salary, PF & ESI¶

    Q89. What is the employee's EPF contribution rate in the lesson?

    Answer: 12% of basic + DA

    The employer also contributes 12%, split between EPF and pension.

    Q90. What are the ESI contribution rates in the lesson?

    Answer: Employee 0.75% and employer 3.25% of gross wages

    ESI applies to employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month.

    Q91. Which voucher records daily attendance in TallyPrime payroll?

    Answer: Attendance voucher (Ctrl+F5)

    The Payroll voucher then calculates earnings and deductions with auto fill.

    Q92. What are pay heads?

    Answer: Components of salary such as Basic, HRA and deductions like PF

    They define earnings and deductions.

    04 · Advanced Inventory: Batches, Godowns & Valuation¶

    Q93. How do you move 100 fans from the Main Warehouse to Nashik Depot?

    Answer: Stock Journal (Alt+F7) → Transfer

    Godown Summary shows stock by location afterward.

    Q94. With opening 100 @ ₹1,800, a purchase of 200 @ ₹1,950 and 220 sold, what is the FIFO closing stock?

    Answer: 80 @ ₹1,950 = ₹1,56,000

    FIFO sells the older 100 @ 1,800 first, then 120 @ 1,950, leaving 80 @ 1,950.

    Q95. What is the weighted-average cost per unit in that example?

    Answer: ₹1,900

    (1,80,000 + 3,90,000) ÷ 300 = 1,900.

    Q96. What does FEFO mean for perishables?

    Answer: First expiry, first out

    Select batches on sale by earliest expiry.

    05 · Manufacturing: Bill of Materials & Job Work¶

    Q97. What does a Bill of Materials define?

    Answer: The components needed per unit of a finished item

    For one study table: plywood, legs, screws, laminate and drawer runners.

    Q98. Producing 20 tables needs 4 legs each. How many legs?

    Answer: 80

    20 × 4 = 80.

    Q99. How is the finished goods cost computed?

    Answer: (Components + additional costs such as labour and power) ÷ quantity

    For 20 tables: components plus ₹12,000 labour and ₹3,000 power.

    Q100. Under GST, how do goods move for job work?

    Answer: On a delivery challan without tax, returning within 1 year (inputs) or 3 years (capital goods)

    Otherwise it's treated as a supply; movements are reported in ITC-04.

    06 · Banking: Cheques, e-Payments & Post-Dated Cheques¶

    Q101. How does TallyPrime print a cheque?

    Answer: In a Payment voucher choose transaction type Cheque; it picks the next number and prints the amount in words

    Enable cheque printing in the bank ledger first.

    Q102. What do post-dated cheques do until their date?

    Answer: They don't affect balances until the date; Post-Dated Summary lists upcoming items

    On the due date the voucher becomes regular automatically.

    Q103. What is a maker-checker control in e-payments?

    Answer: One person creates the payments, another approves the upload in net banking

    It reduces payment fraud and errors.

    Q104. What does the Payment Advice report send to parties?

    Answer: Details of payments made, such as invoices settled and TDS deducted

    Email it with Alt+M.

    07 · Foreign Currency & Exports¶

    Q105. 500 fans at $28 with an exchange rate of ₹84.50 gives what invoice value?

    Answer: ₹11,83,000

    $14,000 × 84.50.

    Q106. The customer pays $14,000 when the rate is ₹85.10. What exchange gain?

    Answer: ₹8,400

    14,000 × (85.10 − 84.50) = 8,400, booked as forex gain under Indirect Incomes.

    Q107. What are the two GST routes for exporting goods?

    Answer: Under LUT without paying IGST, or with IGST paid then refunded

    Exports are zero-rated supplies.

    Q108. How is customs duty treated for imports?

    Answer: It becomes part of inventory cost (IGST on imports is claimable as ITC)

    The Bill of Entry gives the IGST paid.

    08 · Year-End Adjustments: Depreciation & Provisions¶

    Q109. March electricity of ₹14,000 is incurred but unpaid. What is the entry?

    Answer: Dr Electricity / Cr Outstanding Expenses

    Outstanding expenses are a current liability.

    Q110. Insurance ₹36,000 paid for July–June leaves how much prepaid at 31 March?

    Answer: ₹9,000

    April–June is 3 of 12 months: 36,000 × 3/12.

    Q111. A 5% provision on ₹3,20,000 of debts over 90 days is how much?

    Answer: ₹16,000

    It's booked as Dr Bad Debts / Cr Provision for doubtful debts.

    Q112. What is the depreciation entry in a Journal voucher?

    Answer: Dr Depreciation (Indirect Expenses) / Cr the asset

    Maintain a fixed asset register with cost, additions, disposals and depreciation.

    09 · GSTR-9 Annual Return & Audit Readiness¶

    Q113. What is GSTR-9C?

    Answer: A self-certified reconciliation between audited financial statements and GSTR-9, for larger taxpayers

    It's required above the lesson's turnover threshold of ₹5 crore.

    Q114. What do composition taxpayers file instead?

    Answer: GSTR-4

    E-commerce operators file GSTR-9B.

    Q115. How should you handle an additional liability discovered while preparing GSTR-9?

    Answer: Pay it with interest through DRC-03 before filing

    Compare TallyPrime's figures with GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the portal.

    Q116. How long should GST records be kept?

    Answer: At least 72 months from the due date of the annual return

    Keep invoices, notes, e-way bills, challans, ITC documents and payment proofs.

    10 · Project — Quarter-End Close for a Manufacturer¶

    Q117. By when are PF/ESI payments made in the project?

    Answer: By the 15th (of the following month)

    TDS deposits are by the 7th.

    Q118. Which transaction combines import, customs duty and IGST in the project?

    Answer: A raw material purchase in USD

    Customs duty becomes part of inventory cost; IGST on imports is claimable.

    Q119. Why does the project include a scrap sale?

    Answer: To practise TCS collection

    TCS applies to specified sales such as scrap.

    Q120. What does the close include after adjustments?

    Answer: Stock verification, bank reconciliation, GST returns and a management report pack

    The deliverable checklist lists them all.

    Level 4 · Master¶

    01 · Financial Statement Analysis & Ratios¶

    Q121. Sales 600, COGS 450. What is the gross profit margin?

    Answer: 25%

    (600 − 450) ÷ 600.

    Q122. Current assets 180 (stock 90) and current liabilities 110. What is the quick ratio?

    Answer: About 0.82

    (180 − 90) ÷ 110.

    Q123. What does the cash conversion cycle of 72 days mean?

    Answer: Each rupee of sales is tied up for over two months

    DSO 46 + inventory days 73 − creditor days 47.

    Q124. Why can a profitable business still run out of cash?

    Answer: Profit isn't cash: stock and debtors tie cash up, so check the cash flow report

    Compare cash from operations with profit.

    02 · MIS Reports for Management¶

    Q125. What does an MIS summary dashboard show?

    Answer: Revenue, gross margin %, net profit, cash and bank, receivables, payables and stock, with change vs last month and budget

    It is a one-page view.

    Q126. How is a cash flow forecast built?

    Answer: Opening cash + expected receipts (including post-dated cheques) − planned payments

    Planned payments include suppliers, salaries, GST, TDS and EMIs.

    Q127. When should the monthly MIS pack be delivered?

    Answer: Within the first 5–7 working days, after reconciliation

    Keep definitions such as 'gross margin' consistent.

    Q128. What should accompany each section of the pack?

    Answer: 2–3 bullets on what changed, why and the recommended action

    Commentary drives action.

    03 · Internal Controls, Security & the Edit Log¶

    Q129. What is segregation of duties?

    Answer: The person who records purchases shouldn't approve payments or reconcile the bank

    It reduces error and fraud.

    Q130. What does the edit log record?

    Answer: Creation, alteration and deletion of vouchers and masters with user, date, time and previous versions

    View it via Related Reports → Edit Log.

    Q131. Why lock periods after closing?

    Answer: So no one alters closed periods; changes need administrator unlock and leave a trace

    For example lock up to 31 March after audit.

    Q132. Which role can create vouchers only?

    Answer: Data Entry

    An Auditor can only view.

    04 · Data Management: Backup, Restore & Split¶

    Q133. What is the 3-2-1 backup rule?

    Answer: 3 copies, on 2 different media, 1 off-site

    For example encrypted cloud storage as the off-site copy.

    Q134. Why test restores regularly?

    Answer: A backup you can't restore isn't a backup

    Restore to a different path first if unsure.

    Q135. What must you do before repairing or splitting a company?

    Answer: Back it up first

    Then verify closing balances equal opening balances in the new company after a split.

    Q136. Why should you never copy company files while TallyPrime has the company open?

    Answer: Use TallyPrime's own backup tools instead

    Files may be inconsistent while open.

    05 · The Annual Compliance Calendar¶

    Q137. By which day is monthly GSTR-1 due and by which is GSTR-3B?

    Answer: GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th

    TDS/TCS is deposited by the 7th and PF/ESI by the 15th.

    Q138. Which are the quarterly TDS statement due dates?

    Answer: 31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May

    15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March are advance tax instalments.

    Q139. When is GSTR-9/9C due?

    Answer: 31 December

    It follows the financial year.

    Q140. How should you run the calendar?

    Answer: Recurring reminders 5 and 2 days before each deadline, with an owner and a backup

    Maintain a compliance tracker.

    06 · Accounting for Startups & MSMEs¶

    Q141. Which structure has limited liability with lighter compliance than a company?

    Answer: LLP

    A private limited company is a separate legal entity with more compliance.

    Q142. A founder lends money to the company without being issued shares. How is it recorded?

    Answer: As an unsecured loan with documentation, not capital

    Personal expenses through the business are recorded as drawings.

    Q143. ₹60 lakh cash with ₹5 lakh monthly net burn gives what runway?

    Answer: 12 months

    Runway is cash balance ÷ net burn.

    Q144. What is the maximum period for a buyer to pay an MSME supplier?

    Answer: 45 days

    The lesson notes delayed payment protection and tracking MSME status in ledgers.

    07 · Moving to an ERP: Tally, Zoho, SAP & Oracle¶

    Q145. Which is a sign you may need more than TallyPrime alone?

    Answer: Many users across locations needing real-time access, complex approvals, MRP or multi-company consolidation

    Moving is a business decision, not a default.

    Q146. How should you evaluate an ERP?

    Answer: Prioritized requirements, 5-year total cost of ownership and demos using your own scenarios

    Check partner experience and people readiness too.

    Q147. What do you do before migrating from TallyPrime?

    Answer: Clean the data: remove duplicate ledgers, fix groupings and close old bills

    Choose a cut-over date, usually a financial year or quarter start.

    Q148. Which system does the lesson pair with cloud-first SMEs and startups?

    Answer: Zoho Books / Zoho One

    S/4HANA and Fusion are for large enterprises.

    08 · Running an Accounting Practice¶

    Q149. Which services are reserved for Chartered Accountants or other authorised professionals?

    Answer: Statutory audits, tax audits and certification, among others

    Check what you're allowed to offer.

    Q150. What does an engagement letter set out?

    Answer: Scope, client responsibilities, timelines, fees, payment terms, confidentiality and limitations

    It protects both sides.

    Q151. How should client data be handled?

    Answer: Secure remote access, encryption on personal devices, backups and clear data-return terms

    DPDP obligations apply to personal data.

    Q152. Why niche by industry?

    Answer: To work faster and market better

    For example restaurants, traders, clinics or startups.

    09 · Career Paths: Accountant to Finance Manager¶

    Q153. Which role leads a team and owns budgeting, cash flow and banking relationships?

    Answer: Accounts Manager / Finance Manager

    The CFO/Financial Controller role adds strategy, funding and governance.

    Q154. Which skills do employers look for?

    Answer: TallyPrime with GST/TDS/payroll, end-to-end GST, advanced Excel and month-end discipline

    ERP exposure helps for larger companies.

    Q155. How should resume achievements read?

    Answer: Results-focused, e.g. reconciled GSTR-2B monthly and recovered missed ITC

    Quantify honestly.

    Q156. How should you build a portfolio from the course?

    Answer: Use the projects with exported reports (monthly books, GST month, quarter-end close, full-year capstone)

    Practical experience matters as much as certificates.

    10 · Capstone — A Full Year of Books & Compliance¶

    Q157. How much of the year does the capstone require you to record in full?

    Answer: At least one representative month per quarter, summarising the others

    There are four representative months.

    Q158. Which year-end items does the capstone include?

    Answer: Adjustments, forex restatement, GSTR-9 reconciliation and ITC reversals for unpaid suppliers

    It ends by locking the year, backing up and splitting.

    Q159. What controls are set at the start?

    Answer: Security roles, the edit log, a backup policy and a compliance calendar

    Part of setup in the brief.

    Q160. What does the annual MIS report include?

    Answer: Recommendations against the budget

    It sits in the deliverables checklist.

    Mixed questions across all levels¶

    Q161. The owner puts ₹5,00,000 cash into a new business. What is the entry?

    Answer: Cash A/c Dr / To Capital A/c

    Cash comes in (debit) and Capital is the giver (credit).

    Q162. You deposit cash into the bank. Which voucher type?

    Answer: Contra

    Contra covers transfers between cash and bank.

    Q163. An adjustment such as depreciation, with no cash or bank, needs which voucher?

    Answer: Journal

    Rule of thumb: cash/bank involved → Payment/Receipt/Contra, otherwise Journal.

    Q164. A customer's account shows a credit balance and a supplier's shows a debit balance in the Trial Balance. What should you do?

    Answer: Investigate: these oddities suggest wrong entries or overpayment

    Scan for oddities after confirming debits equal credits.

    Q165. You sell from Telangana to a registered buyer in Karnataka at 18%. Which tax is charged?

    Answer: IGST 18%

    Inter-state supply attracts IGST.

    Q166. Your purchase invoice from a supplier doesn't appear in GSTR-2B. What should you do with the ITC?

    Answer: Hold it back until the supplier reports it, and follow up

    ITC requires the supplier to have reported the invoice.

    Q167. You issued an invoice with the wrong quantity. How do you correct it under GST?

    Answer: Issue a credit or debit note referencing the original invoice

    Never edit an issued invoice's tax value.

    Q168. Your turnover is above ₹5 crore and you raise a B2B sale. What must you do?

    Answer: Report it to the IRP and get an IRN, with a QR code on the invoice

    E-invoicing applies to B2B invoices above the threshold.

    Q169. You hire an advocate and pay ₹50,000 plus 18% GST under reverse charge. Who pays the GST and how?

    Answer: You, in cash through the electronic cash ledger, then claim ITC if eligible

    RCM tax must be paid in cash.

    Q170. A customer pays ₹70,000 against two open invoices. Which entry type pairs payment to bills?

    Answer: Agst Ref against each bill

    Agst Ref settles specific bills.

    Q171. You must see profit by branch and by salesperson. What do you use?

    Answer: Cost categories and cost centres

    Allocate in vouchers and use Cost Centre reports.

    Q172. You pay a contractor ₹1,00,000 and the payee has no PAN. What generally applies?

    Answer: A higher TDS rate (often 20%)

    Invalid or missing PAN triggers a higher rate.

    Q173. By when is TDS deducted in a month generally deposited?

    Answer: The 7th of the following month

    For March it is 30 April.

    Q174. Copper wire: opening 100 @ ₹1,800, purchase 200 @ ₹1,950, 220 sold. Under FIFO, what is the cost of goods sold?

    Answer: ₹4,14,000

    100 × 1,800 + 120 × 1,950.

    Q175. You send tables to a polishing unit. How do you move the goods under GST?

    Answer: Delivery challan without tax, with Material Out/In vouchers

    Track return within the allowed time and report ITC-04.

    Q176. A foreign customer pays at a better exchange rate than the invoice rate. How is the difference treated?

    Answer: As an exchange gain under Indirect Incomes

    Book it with a forex gain/loss adjustment.

    Q177. It is year end. March electricity of ₹14,000 hasn't been paid. What do you book?

    Answer: Dr Electricity / Cr Outstanding Expenses

    An adjusting entry for an expense incurred but not paid.

    Q178. A profitable business is short of cash. Which ratios and reports help you diagnose it?

    Answer: Cash conversion cycle, debtor days and the cash flow report

    Profit isn't cash; stock and debtors tie it up.

    Q179. Your accounting data is on one PC. What is the safe backup approach?

    Answer: 3 copies, on 2 media, 1 off-site (e.g. encrypted cloud), with tested restores

    A backup you can't restore isn't a backup.

    Q180. A junior accountant records purchases and also approves payments and reconciles the bank. What is the control weakness?

    Answer: No segregation of duties

    Separate recording, approval and reconciliation, and use the edit log.
